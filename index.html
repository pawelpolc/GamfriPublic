Our Mission

Gamfri was born out of a simple idea: gaming should be accessible, private, and fun for everyone. As an independent developer, my goal is to strip away the noise of modern mobile gaming—the endless logins, the data tracking, and the aggressive monetization.

I am building a platform where the only thing that matters is the gameplay. Every game added to the Gamfri ecosystem is hand-picked to ensure it provides immediate fun and lasting value, all while respecting your time and your privacy.
