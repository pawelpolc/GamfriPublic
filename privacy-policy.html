Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gamfri ("the App"), operated by Dotsoft Paweł Polc ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information in relation to your use of our mobile application.

1. No Personal Data Collection

The App is designed to be fully functional offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. There is no account registration required to use Gamfri.

2. Local Storage

Any game-related data, such as high scores, unlocked levels, or settings, is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to our servers. If you delete the App, this local data will typically be removed by your operating system.

3. Third-Party Services

The App is distributed via the Google Play Store. While we do not collect data, Google may collect certain information through Google Play Services for the purpose of app updates, security, and basic diagnostic analytics (such as crash reports). We encourage you to review the Google Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, it is inherently safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We recommend checking this page periodically for any changes.
