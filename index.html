Our Mission

Gamfri was born out of a simple idea: gaming should be accessible, private, and fun for everyone. My goal is to strip away the noise of modern mobile gaming — "pay-to-win", the data tracking, and the aggressive monetization.

I am building a platform where the only thing that matters is the gameplay. Every game added to the Gamfri ecosystem is hand-picked to ensure it provides immediate fun and lasting value, all while respecting your time and your privacy.

Game development has always been a great passion of mine, and I do it in my free time. As a father of two amazing sons, I want to give them the opportunity to play freely — without ads or in-game payments. Every child deserves that, and adults do too.
